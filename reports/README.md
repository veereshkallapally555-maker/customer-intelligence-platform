# Reports

This folder contains business reports and visualizations generated from the cleaned e-commerce data.

## Reports

- `business_kpis.csv` — Overall business KPIs such as total orders, total revenue, and average order value.
- `customer_summary.csv` — Customer-level order and revenue metrics.
- `product_performance.csv` — Top products by revenue and units sold.

## Charts

The `charts/` folder contains visualizations for:

- Monthly revenue
- Revenue by category
- Revenue by city
- Top customers by revenue
- Top products by revenue
